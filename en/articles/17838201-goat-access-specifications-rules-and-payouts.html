GOAT Access: specifications, rules and payouts

Updated 2 November 2026

GOAT Access is a one-phase 50K evaluation with the lowest entry price at GFF: $39 to start, and a one-time $99 activation fee once you pass. There is no time limit, no daily loss limit and no consistency rule in the evaluation.

Key benefits

  • $39 to start the evaluation
  • No time limit and no minimum trading days. You can pass as soon as you hit the target
  • No daily loss limit, in evaluation or funded
  • No consistency rule in the evaluation
  • End of day trailing max drawdown, which locks at $50,100
  • Weekly payouts, up to $1,500 per payout
  • 80/20 profit split

GOAT Access is offered on 50K only. It is sold on its own page and is not listed in the dashboard's plan selector. You can hold up to 3 funded GOAT Access accounts at the same time, alongside your other GFF plans.

Two things to know before you buy

  • There is an activation fee. GOAT Access is the only GFF plan with one. You pay $99 once you pass, before your funded account is issued.
  • High impact news trading is not allowed, in the evaluation or on the funded account. This is stricter than Flex and EOD, which allow news trading during the evaluation.

How to pass

  • Reach the profit target of 5% ($2,500).
  • Stay above the 4% ($2,000) end of day trailing drawdown, and within the trading rules below.

There is no minimum number of trading days and no time limit. When you pass for the first time, you complete KYC before your funded account is issued.

Maximum drawdown (end of day trailing)

Your maximum drawdown is $2,000 (4%) and trails your end of day balance. It applies in both the evaluation and funded phases. Breaching it is a hard breach, which closes the account.

  • Your drawdown floor starts at $48,000.
  • It moves up only when you close a trading day at a new high balance.
  • Once your end of day balance reaches $52,100, the floor locks at $50,100 (your starting balance + $100) and stops trailing.

Daily loss limit

None. GOAT Access has no daily loss limit in the evaluation or on the funded account, and no maximum daily profit cap.

Consistency rule

  • Evaluation: none.
  • Funded: 35%. It is checked when you request a payout.

Payouts

  • Eligibility: 5 profitable days of at least $150 (0.3%) each.
  • Buffer: your balance must clear the $2,100 (4.2%) buffer plus the $500 minimum payout.
  • Minimum request: $500.
  • Maximum request: $1,500 per payout, the same cap on every payout.
  • Payout cycle: weekly, once you meet the payout rules again for each request.

Example. You start at $50,000. Once your balance reaches $52,600 or more, with 5 days of $150+ profit, you can request your first payout.

Profit split

80% to you, 20% to GFF.

Holding time: the 1 minute rule

At each payout request, both of the following must be true for that payout cycle:

  • at least 50% of your closed trades were held longer than 1 minute, and
  • at least 50% of your net profit came from trades held longer than 1 minute.

If either is not met, the payout request is declined and the profit from trades held under 1 minute in that cycle is deducted from the account. This is not an account breach, and your account stays open.

News trading

Trading high impact news is not allowed, in the evaluation or on the funded account.

Overnight and weekend holding

Not permitted. All positions must be closed before the market closes.

Platforms

GOAT Access is available on DxFeed platforms (Quantower, ATAS, DeepCharts) and TickBlaze. NinjaTrader, Tradovate and TradeSea are not available on GOAT Access.

What happens if I breach?

The evaluation ends. You can reset for $39 or buy a new evaluation.
