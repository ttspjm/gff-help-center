Can I reset my Funded account?

Updated 8 September 2026

Yes, funded account resets are available, with the following conditions.

Eligibility

  • Available only on EOD 50K and 100K funded accounts
  • Not available on 1-Day-Pass-Plan, Instant Classic, Instant Lite, Daily Payouts, or other EOD account sizes
  • Available only before your first payout. Once an approved withdrawal has been processed on the account, the reset option is permanently blocked

Pricing

  • 50K EOD: $499 (1st reset) / $599 (2nd reset)
  • 100K EOD: $599 (1st reset) / $699 (2nd reset)

Limits

Maximum 2 resets per account.

Daily Payouts

Daily Payouts funded accounts cannot be reset. If a funded account is breached, a new evaluation must be purchased. Daily Payouts evaluations can be reset twice in the normal way.

Why the reset option may not appear on your account

  • The account isn't a 50K or 100K EOD funded account
  • You've already received an approved payout
  • You've already used both resets
  • The account is closed for a reason that doesn't qualify for reset

If you believe you're eligible but don't see the option, contact support.
