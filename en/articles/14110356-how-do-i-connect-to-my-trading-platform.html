How do I connect to my trading platform?

Updated 19 October 2026

After purchasing your account, you will receive login credentials via email. Use these credentials to connect to your chosen platform. Detailed step-by-step connection guides for each platform are available in our help center at help.goatfundedfutures.com.

If you chose TradeSea, see Can I trade on TradeSea?
