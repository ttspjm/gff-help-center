Profit Vault: What happens to my unwithdrawn sim profit?

Updated 19 November 2026

What is the Profit Vault?

The Profit Vault holds the sim profit you had not yet withdrawn when you moved to live. It is kept for you and unlocked as you make profit on your live account.

How do I unlock it?

For every $10,000 of profit you make on live, $1,000 is unlocked from your vault, until the vault is empty. Example: a vault of $3,000 is fully unlocked once you have made $30,000 of profit on live.

Does it change my profit split?

No. You keep 90% of your live profit from the first dollar. Vault unlocks are paid on top of that.

How is it paid?

Each $1,000 is paid together with the payout that takes you over that $10,000 milestone. The 3% Rise fee applies.

I had several sim accounts. Do I get one vault?

Yes. Unwithdrawn profit from every account combined into your live account goes into one Profit Vault.

What happens to my vault if my live account breaches or I take the cash-out?

Your Profit Vault is voided, as you will be going back into a challenge.
