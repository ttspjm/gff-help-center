Is there a daily loss limit?

Updated 25 November 2026

This depends on your account type.

EOD Challenge

  • Evaluation: none.
  • Funded: 2.5% of your starting balance, so $1,250 on a 50K, $2,500 on a 100K, $3,750 on a 150K.

1-Day-Pass

Optional at checkout. If you choose it, it applies in both phases, at the same percentage in each, and hitting it closes the account.

  • 25K: 2.4%, which is $600
  • 50K: 2%, which is $1,000
  • 100K: 1.5%, which is $1,500

Instant

Optional at checkout. If you choose it: $500 on a 25K, $1,000 on a 50K, $1,500 on a 100K, $2,250 on a 150K. Hitting it closes the account.

Instant Classic (bought before 18 November 2026)

3% of your starting balance, so $750 on a 25K, $1,500 on a 50K, $3,000 on a 100K, $4,500 on a 150K.

Instant Lite

  • 25K: none
  • 50K: 2%, which is $1,000
  • 100K: 2%, which is $2,000

Flex Challenge

None by default, in either phase. If you turn on the daily loss limit option at checkout, it is $1,000 on a 50K, $1,500 on a 100K and $2,500 on a 150K. It applies in both phases, and hitting it closes the account. See below.

GOAT Daily

Optional at checkout. If you choose it, it applies in both phases, and hitting it closes the account.

  • 25K: 2%, which is $500
  • 50K: 2%, which is $1,000
  • 100K: 1.5%, which is $1,500
  • 150K: 1.5%, which is $2,250

What happens when you reach it

On EOD funded accounts, hitting the daily loss limit is a soft breach, not a hard one. Your open positions are closed and the account is paused for the rest of the trading day. It resumes automatically on the next trading day, and the account is not closed. On GOAT Daily, 1-Day-Pass, Instant and Flex the daily loss limit is optional at checkout, and hitting it closes the account on the first hit.

Accounts bought before 18 November 2026: on 1-Day-Pass, Instant Classic, Instant Lite and GOAT Daily the daily loss limit is a soft breach. GOAT Daily closes the account on a third hit within any rolling 30 day period, and Instant Lite on a third hit over the life of the account.

Flex with the daily loss limit option

On a Flex account bought with the daily loss limit option, the first hit closes the account, in the evaluation or funded. There is no pause and no strike count. See Can I add a daily loss limit to my Flex account?

For the exact figure on your account, check your dashboard.
