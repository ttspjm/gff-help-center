Cash-out: Can I take a payout instead of going live?

Updated 19 November 2026

What is the cash-out option?

When you are approved for live, you can take 50% of your live drawdown as an instant payout instead of trading the live account. It is completely optional.

How much is the cash-out?

  • 50K Live: $1,000
  • 100K Live: $1,500
  • 150K Live: $2,250

The amount is based on your combined live size, which is capped at 150K.

How do I choose it?

Your approval email sets out both options. Reply to the email with the one you want.

How is it paid?

It is processed like a normal payout. The full amount is paid without a profit split, and the 3% Rise fee still applies.

What happens after I cash out?

Your eligible sim accounts close and a 21-day cooldown begins straight away. After the cooldown you can start a new challenge and work your way back to live.

What happens to my Profit Vault if I cash out?

Your Profit Vault is voided, as you will be going back into a challenge.

Do I have to take it?

No. You can go live instead and trade real GFF capital with daily uncapped payouts and a 90% profit share.
