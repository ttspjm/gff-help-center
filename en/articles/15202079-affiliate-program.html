Affiliate Program

Updated 14 November 2026

You earn commission on every sale made through your affiliate link. You also earn 5% of the sales made by affiliates you invite, called sub-affiliates.

How to join

Apply on our Affiliate Program page. Our team reviews every application. Once you're approved, you'll get your affiliate link and your invite link.

Your commission

  • 12% on your sales until you have referred 100 different customers.
  • 15% on every sale from your 100th customer onward.

The 100 counts different customers, not purchases. If one customer buys three accounts within 30 days of clicking your link, each purchase earns commission, but that customer counts once toward the 100. Customers you referred before the new rates also count.

Challenge purchases and add-ons earn commission. Resets don't.

Commission is calculated on the price the customer paid, after any discount. Your own purchases don't earn commission.

There is no separate rate for your first sale.

Sub-affiliate commission

Invite other creators, influencers or traders to apply to the affiliate program with your invite link. Everyone who is approved after applying through it becomes your sub-affiliate, and you earn 5% of the full amount of every sale they make.

Your sub-affiliates still earn their own full 12% or 15% on those sales. Your 5% doesn't come out of their commission.

You earn the 5% for as long as your sub-affiliate's account stays active. If their affiliate account is disabled for any reason, including fraud, the 5% stops.

Someone only becomes your sub-affiliate by applying through your invite link. An affiliate who already joined without it can't be linked to you unless they apply again through your link.

There is no limit on how many sub-affiliates you can invite. The 5% covers one level only, so you don't earn on affiliates that your sub-affiliates invite.

Your invite link is in your affiliate dashboard.

Example

Say you invite 10 creators and each of them makes $2,000 in sales this month. That's $20,000 in total, and 5% of it is $1,000 for you, without selling anything extra yourself.

If your sub-affiliates grow and sell more, your 5% grows with them.

When you can request your commission

You can request a commission 30 days after it's credited to your account. Each commission has its own 30 days. For example, commission credited on 13 November can be requested from 13 December.

The minimum payout request is $100. You need to complete identity verification (KYC) before your first payout. Payouts are sent by crypto or Skrill and usually take 2 days to process.

Cookie tracking

We use a 30-day cookie. If someone clicks your link and buys within 30 days, the sale counts toward your commission. Purchases after those 30 days don't count. For example, if someone clicks your link and buys on day 29 and again on day 31, only the first purchase earns commission.

Refunds and chargebacks

If a sale you referred is refunded or charged back, the commission on it is removed, even if its 30 days have already passed. If it was already paid out, it's deducted from your balance.

We may disable the affiliate account of affiliates with a high number of refunds or chargebacks.
