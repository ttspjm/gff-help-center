How do I know if my account is active and ready to trade?

Updated 28 July 2026

Log into your GFF dashboard at app.goatfundedfutures.com. Your active accounts will be listed along with their status, credentials, and current balance. If the account shows as "Active" with login credentials visible, you're ready to connect your platform and start trading.

If you have any questions about your account status, reach out to our support team at support@goatfundedfutures.com with the relevant details and screenshots.
