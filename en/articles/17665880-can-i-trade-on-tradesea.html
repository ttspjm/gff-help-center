Can I trade on TradeSea?

Updated 19 October 2026

Yes. TradeSea is available on every plan. It is a futures trading platform that runs in your browser, with TradingView charts, a DOM, bracket orders and built-in risk settings. It also has apps on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Choosing TradeSea at checkout

At checkout, under Select provider, choose Tradesea. TradeSea is its own provider, separate from CQG, DxFeed and TickBlaze Market Data.

After payment

Check your email for your account details and how to connect. Your account also appears in your dashboard at app.goatfundedfutures.com.

If you can't connect, contact our support team with your account ID and a screenshot of the error.

Copy trading

Our copy trading rules are the same on TradeSea as on every other platform. See Signals, copy trading and automated strategies for the full rules.
