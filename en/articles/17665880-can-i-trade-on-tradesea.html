Can I trade on TradeSea?

Updated 21 October 2026

Yes. TradeSea is available on every plan. It is a futures trading platform that runs in your browser, with TradingView charts, a DOM, bracket orders and built-in risk settings. It also has apps on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Choosing TradeSea at checkout

At checkout, under Select provider, choose Tradesea. TradeSea is its own provider, separate from CQG, DxFeed and TickBlaze Market Data.

As with every provider, you cannot move an account to or from TradeSea after purchase. Make sure it is the platform you want before you pay.

Your login details

After payment you receive an email with your username, password and server, and a link to log in to TradeSea. Keep this email. You need it to connect.

Connecting your account

  1. Open the TradeSea link in your email. Sign in with Google, or with your email address. If you use email, TradeSea sends you a one-time code.
  2. Open the Account Centre and click Connect New.
  3. Find Goat Funded Futures in the list and click Connect.
  4. Enter the username and password from your email exactly as given. If TradeSea asks for a server or environment, choose the one named in your email.
  5. Click Connect. Your account now shows as Connected in the Account Centre.

If the connection fails

  • Your credentials are rejected. Usernames and passwords are case-sensitive, so copy and paste them from the email. Use the login from the credentials email, not your GFF dashboard login.
  • Goat Funded Futures is not in the list. Contact our support team with your account ID.
  • The account says Connected but does not appear. Refresh the page, reopen Station, and pick the account from the selector in the top-left corner.

If none of these fixes it, contact our support team with your account ID and a screenshot of the error.

Market data

TradeSea comes with free top of the book (L1) market data for the full CME bundle, the same as CQG. That covers CME, CBOT, NYMEX and COMEX, so metals and energy are included without asking us to add them.

Other Rithmic platforms

Your TradeSea login is a Rithmic login. It also works on other Rithmic platforms, such as Sierra Chart and R|Trader Pro, as long as the platform lists our server, GOAT FundedFutures. If a platform does not list it, that platform cannot connect to your account.

Copy trading with SecureCopy

TradeSea has its own copier, SecureCopy. You can use it to copy trades between your own accounts. Copying another trader is not allowed, on TradeSea or anywhere else.

SecureCopy copies one leader account to up to 10 follower accounts per group, with up to 3 groups. Your plan's copy limits still apply. Daily Payouts accounts can only copy to and from other Daily Payouts accounts. Instant Lite allows copying across up to 3 accounts of the same program and size.

Two SecureCopy settings need care:

  • Multipliers. Each follower can copy at 1x, 2x or 3x the leader's size. A follower at 2x places twice as many contracts as the leader, so check that every follower stays inside the contract limit for its plan and size.
  • Risk settings on followers. Saving a copy group clears the daily loss, daily profit and position bracket settings on the follower accounts in TradeSea. Your GFF rules do not change. We still enforce your daily loss limit and drawdown on every account.

SecureCopy runs on TradeSea's servers, so it keeps copying after you close the browser tab. That is allowed and does not count as a VPS.
