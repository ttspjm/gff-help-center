The daily loss limit on Daily Payouts

Updated 19 November 2026

The daily loss limit on Daily Payouts is optional. You choose at checkout whether to trade with it or without it, and the choice carries into your funded account.

The limit, if you choose it

It applies in both the evaluation and the funded phase. It is the most you may lose in a single trading day.

Account sizeDaily loss limit
25K$500 (2%)
50K$1,000 (2%)
100K$1,500 (1.5%)
150K$2,250 (1.5%)

What happens when you hit it

Hitting it is a hard breach. The account is closed on the first hit, in the evaluation or funded. There is no pause and no strike count.

Accounts bought without the daily loss limit have no daily loss limit. Your maximum drawdown still applies.

Can I remove the daily loss limit?

Yes, at checkout. Daily Payouts can be bought with or without the daily loss limit, and the choice carries into the funded account.

Accounts bought before 18 November 2026

On these accounts the daily loss limit is always on. Hitting it is a soft breach: your open positions are closed and trading is paused for the rest of the day, and you can trade again in the next session.

The third hit within any rolling 30 day period is a hard breach and closes the account. It is a rolling window, not a calendar month: every hit stays on the count for 30 days from the day it happened, then drops off. Your dashboard shows how many hits you have in the current window.
