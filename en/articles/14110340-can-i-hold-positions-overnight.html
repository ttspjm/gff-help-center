Can I hold positions overnight?

Updated 8 September 2026

All positions must be closed before the end of the trading session. This applies to every account type: EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan, Instant Classic, Instant Lite, Flex and Daily Payouts. Any position still open is closed automatically at 15:55 CT, five minutes before the session ends.

Weekend holding is also not permitted.
