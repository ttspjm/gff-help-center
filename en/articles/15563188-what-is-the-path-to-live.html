What is the Path to Live?

Updated 17 August 2026

The Path to Live is how GFF rewards its most consistent traders with the next step in their journey: a live brokerage account, trading real capital that we provide. Once you qualify, your simulated funded account becomes a live account, you trade with real GFF capital, and you keep 90% of every withdrawal — with the freedom to request payouts daily and no per-payout caps. It is the opportunity to turn proven sim performance into a long-term partnership trading live.

Who is the Path to Live for?

Every GFF trader. The opportunity is open across every product — EOD Challenge, 1-Day-Pass-Plan, Instant Classic, and Flex Challenge — and every account size. There is nothing extra to buy and nothing to sign up for: your place is earned purely through your trading on the sim funded account you already hold.

Why does GFF offer this?

Because we want to grow alongside the traders who prove they have a genuine edge. The Path to Live is how GFF backs its best performers with real capital on a live brokerage account — the structure used by the most established firms in the industry — and builds a lasting partnership rather than a one-off relationship. For you, that means trading real GFF capital, daily uncapped payouts, and a clear runway to scale your results over time.

When does live trading launch?

We are in the final stages of launching our live broker accounts, with the live environment expected to go live in Summer 2026 — so this is the perfect time to be building toward qualification.

What if I qualify before the live environment is ready?

Qualifying early is great news — it means you have already proven the consistency we are looking for, and your live account is being reserved for you. With the live environment launching imminently, the next step is getting you onto live capital rather than keeping you in the simulator.

If the risk team approves you for transition before the live environment opens, your sim accounts are held while your live account is prepared, so your transition is ready to go the moment we launch. During this short window, trading activity with GFF is paused across your accounts while we finalise your live setup.

As soon as the live environment is live, you will be transitioned and trading real GFF capital — with daily uncapped payouts and a 90% profit share waiting on the other side. You have earned your place; this simply makes sure you start where the real opportunity is.
