Can I switch my platform after purchase?

Updated 28 July 2026

Platform switches are only possible within the same market data provider.

CQG

  • Tradovate
  • NinjaTrader

DxFeed

  • Volumetrica
  • Quantower
  • DeepCharts
  • ATAS
  • DeepMap

TickBlaze Market Data

• TickBlaze

Please note that platform changes are only made if the issue is on our end (for example, a technical issue), not for incorrect selections made during checkout.
