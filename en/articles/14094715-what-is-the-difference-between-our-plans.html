Flex Challenge notes

  • Default profit split: 80/20
  • Optional 90/10 add-on available for an additional 20% of the challenge price
  • No microscalping rule. The 1 minute rule applies instead, see the holding time article
  • Drawdown by size: Flex 50K $2,000 (4%), Flex 100K $3,000 (3%), Flex 150K $4,500 (3%)

Daily Payouts notes

  • 90/10 profit split from the first payout, with no add-on to buy
  • Request a payout every 24 hours, with no fixed windows and no winning day count
  • Full contract limits from day one: 2/20 on the 25K, 4/40 on the 50K, 6/60 on the 100K, 8/80 on the 150K
  • The 2 minute microscalping rule does not apply. A 60 second holding rule applies at payout instead, see the holding time article
  • At least 50% of every payout request must be profit made since your last payout
  • Manual trading only. Bots and automated strategies are not permitted
  • Drawdown by size: 25K $1,000 (4%), 50K $2,000 (4%), 100K $3,000 (3%), 150K $4,500 (3%)
  • Full rules in the Daily Payouts specifications

What all plans have in common

  • $0 activation fee.
  • News trading is unrestricted during the evaluation phase on EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan and Flex. Daily Payouts applies the 2 minute buffer in both phases. On all funded accounts a 2 minute buffer applies around high impact news events, with no opening, closing or triggering of pending orders within 2 minutes before or after a scheduled release.
  • No time limit to pass the evaluation on EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan, Flex and Daily Payouts.
