I completed payment but haven't received my login details. What should I do?

Updated 28 July 2026

Try the following steps:

  1. Check your spam or junk email folder.
  2. Make sure the email address on your GFF account matches where you're checking.
  3. Log into your GFF dashboard at app.goatfundedfutures.com — your credentials may already be visible there.

If none of the above works, contact support on support@goatfundedfutures.com with your order confirmation number or payment receipt.
