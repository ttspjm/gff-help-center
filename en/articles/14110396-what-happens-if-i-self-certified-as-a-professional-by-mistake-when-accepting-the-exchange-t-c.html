What happens if I self-certified as a professional by mistake when accepting the Exchange T&C?

Updated 28 July 2026

If you selected Professional status, you will not be able to use your trading account. We are only able to provide market data and accounts to Non-Professional subscribers.

If this was selected by mistake, contact our support team with your account ID so we can assist with the correction process.
