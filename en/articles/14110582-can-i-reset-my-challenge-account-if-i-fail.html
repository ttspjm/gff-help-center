Can I Reset my Challenge account if I fail?

Updated 25 November 2026

Yes. EOD, 1-Day-Pass, Flex and GOAT Daily evaluation accounts can be reset twice, at a discounted price. If you fail an account that has already been reset twice, you will not be able to reset it again and would need to purchase a new challenge.

If your Flex account has the daily loss limit option, the reset account keeps it.

A reset restarts your account balance, profit target, and trading days to zero, giving you a fresh start on the same account.

Instant accounts, including Instant Classic and Instant Lite, cannot be reset. If breached, you would need to purchase a new account.
