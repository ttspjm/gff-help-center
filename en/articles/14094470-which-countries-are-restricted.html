Which countries are restricted?

Updated 7 August 2026

Due to regulatory, licensing, and payment processing restrictions, Goat Funded Futures cannot offer services to residents of the following countries.

Fully Restricted Countries (All Platforms)

Afghanistan, Albania, Antarctica, Algeria, Bosnia, Bulgaria, Bangladesh, Belarus, Burkina Faso, Central African Republic, Chad, Chile, Congo, Côte d'Ivoire, Cuba, Djibouti, East Timor, Ethiopia, Guinea-Bissau, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Iran, Iraq and the Levant (ISIL), Israel, Japan, Jordan, Lao PDR, Lebanon, Lesotho, Libya, Morocco, Malawi, Malaysia, Mali, Myanmar, North Korea, Pakistan, Palestinian Territory, Philippines, Panama, Russia, Romania, Rwanda, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Singapore, Somalia, South Korea, South Sudan, Sri Lanka, Sudan, Syria, Slovenia, Tajikistan, Thailand, Turkey, Togo, Uganda, Venezuela, Vietnam, Yemen, Zimbabwe.

This list is reviewed periodically and may change without notice.

Frequently Asked Question

Can traders from restricted countries participate?

Eligibility to participate depends on applicable laws, regulations, and our compliance requirements.

Goat Funded Futures may restrict or discontinue services in certain countries or regions due to regulatory obligations or changes in business requirements.

If your country becomes unavailable after you purchase an evaluation, our team will contact you directly with the next steps, if applicable.

If you are unsure whether your country is eligible, please contact our Support team before purchasing an account.
