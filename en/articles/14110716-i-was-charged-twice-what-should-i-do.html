I was charged twice, what should I do?

Updated 28 July 2026

Contact our support team immediately at support@goatfundedfutures.com with your order details and payment receipts.

In most cases, duplicate charges are authorization holds that will automatically drop off within 3–5 business days. If an actual duplicate charge has occurred, our team will issue a refund for the extra charge.
