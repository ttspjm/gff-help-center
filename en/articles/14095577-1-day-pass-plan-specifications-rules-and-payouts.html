Everything for 1-Day-Pass-Plan 25K, 50K and 100K: profit target, trailing drawdown with lock, daily loss limits, the profit split, payouts, and what changes once you are funded.

The 1-Day-Pass-Plan Challenge is a one-phase evaluation built for faster payouts. It uses end of day trailing drawdown, pays 80/20 by default, and has no consistency rule during the evaluation.

Key benefits

  • No time limit to pass the evaluation
  • No consistency rule during the evaluation, so you can trade with full flexibility
  • 80/20 profit split, with an optional 90/10 Profit Share Add-On for an extra 20% of the challenge price
  • Payouts every 5 winning days
  • Drawdown lock, so the trailing drawdown stops moving once your balance reaches your starting balance
  • $0 activation fee, so your funded account is activated at no extra cost

Two resets are available per challenge. You can hold up to 5 funded accounts.

How to pass

  1. Reach the profit target of 6% of your starting balance.
  2. Stay within the trading rules and loss limits below.

There is no minimum number of trading days. Once you pass, your evaluation period is reviewed, and your funded account is activated within 2 business days.

What changes when you move from evaluation to funded

Less than you might expect. One rule genuinely changes:

  • The consistency rule starts applying. There is none during the evaluation. On a funded account, no single day may be more than 35% of your total profit for the payout period.

Everything else carries over unchanged: the end of day trailing drawdown and its lock, your maximum drawdown amount, the daily loss limit and its percentages, contract limits, news trading rules, overnight and weekend holding, and the microscalping rule.

Payout rules apply from this point too, since there are no payouts during an evaluation: 80/20 by default, payouts every 5 winning days, and 50% of your available profit per request with the rest staying in the account.

If you bought the 90/10 Profit Share Add-On with your challenge, it carries over automatically. There is nothing to re-purchase. Since there are no payouts during an evaluation, the add-on only affects what you take home once you are funded.

Why does 1-Day-Pass-Plan cost more than EOD?

A 1-Day-Pass-Plan 50K is $109 against $69 for an EOD 50K. What the extra buys:

  • No consistency rule during the evaluation. EOD applies a 50% rule throughout.
  • Faster payouts. 5 winning days instead of 7.
  • A more forgiving funded consistency rule. 35% on 1-Day-Pass-Plan against 30% on EOD, so a bigger single day is allowed.
  • A 25K entry size, which EOD does not offer.

Two tradeoffs worth knowing before you choose:

  • 1-Day-Pass-Plan applies a daily loss limit during the evaluation. EOD does not.
  • 1-Day-Pass-Plan tops out at 100K. EOD goes to 150K.

Maximum drawdown (end of day trailing)

Your maximum drawdown is the level your account balance cannot fall below. It is calculated from your highest end of day balance, not your intraday equity, and it only updates after the trading day closes.

Breaching it is a hard breach, which closes the account immediately. This applies in both the evaluation and funded phases.

Drawdown lock. Once your account balance reaches your starting balance, the trailing drawdown stops moving and locks in place, giving you a fixed safety net for the rest of the account. This applies in both phases.

Consistency rule

  • Evaluation: none. Trade however you like.
  • Funded: no single trading day may account for more than 35% of your total profit during a payout period.

Example on a 1-Day-Pass-Plan 50K. You make $1,750 in one trading day on your funded account. Your total profit for that period needs to reach at least $5,000 before you can request a payout, which puts your best day at 35% of the total.

If you go over the threshold: your account is not terminated. You simply cannot request a payout until your highest profit day falls back to the threshold or below. Keep trading and building profit until it does, then you are eligible again.

Payouts

  • Eligibility: every 5 winning days. A winning day is a net profit of at least 0.2% of your starting balance, so $100 on a 50K.
  • Request window: payout requests are submitted through your dashboard between the 5th and 8th, or the 20th and 23rd, of each month.
  • Profit split: 80/20 by default. You can add the optional 90/10 Profit Share Add-On at checkout for an extra 20% of the challenge price, which is +$15.80 on 25K, +$21.80 on 50K and +$35.80 on 100K.
  • Minimum request: $500, across all account sizes.
  • Maximum request: 50% of your available profit, capped by account size and payout number. The cap rises as you build a track record.

News trading

  • Evaluation: no restrictions.
  • Funded: a 2 minute buffer applies around high impact news events. You cannot open, close, or trigger pending orders within 2 minutes before or after a scheduled release. Positions opened before the window began may be held through the event.

Overnight and weekend holding

  • Overnight holding is not permitted. Any position still open is closed automatically at 15:55 CT, five minutes before the session ends.
  • Weekend holding is not permitted.

Microscalping

Profit from trades closed within 2 minutes of opening is not eligible. It is deducted from your account balance and from any payout calculation. The trade itself is not a breach.

This applies to 1-Day-Pass-Plan accounts in both the evaluation and funded phases. It is separate from the 2 minute news buffer above.

Tick scalping, meaning opening and closing positions within seconds to capture small tick profits, is not permitted on any account. Profit generated this way may be reviewed and deducted.

Inactivity

Place at least one trade every 7 days. If no trades are placed for 7 consecutive days the account is breached and closed, and the fee is not refunded. There is no time limit on passing the evaluation, but the account does need to stay active. If you are going to be away, tell support in advance.
