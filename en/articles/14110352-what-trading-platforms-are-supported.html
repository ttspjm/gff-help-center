What trading platforms are supported?

Updated 7 September 2026

CQG data feed

  • NinjaTrader
  • Tradovate
  • TradingView (via Tradovate)

TickBlaze Market Data

• TickBlaze

DxFeed data feed

  • Quantower
  • ATAS
  • Volumetrica
  • DeepCharts
  • DeepMap

You select your preferred platform and market data provider during checkout.

Important:

Switching between market data providers (CQG, DxFeed, or TickBlaze Market Data) after purchase is not possible due to exchange and licensing costs. Please ensure you select the correct platform and market data provider during checkout.

NinjaTrader and Tradovate country restrictions

NinjaTrader and Tradovate are not available to residents of the countries listed below. These restrictions are in addition to our globally restricted countries (see the full list here), which apply to every platform.

If you select NinjaTrader or Tradovate at checkout and reside in one of these countries, please choose another platform such as TickBlaze, Quantower, or DeepCharts.

Angola, Barbados, Belize, Benin, Botswana, Burundi, Cameroon, Cape Verde, Christmas Island, Cocos (Keeling) Islands, Comoros, Cook Islands, Croatia, Dominica, Equatorial Guinea, Eritrea, Falkland Islands (Malvinas), Fiji, Gabon, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Guyana, Haiti, Heard and McDonald Islands, Jamaica, Kazakhstan, Kenya, Kiribati, Kosovo, Liberia, Malta, Mauritania, Mauritius, Mayotte, Micronesia, Monaco, Mongolia, Montenegro, Montserrat, Mozambique, Namibia, Nauru, Nepal, Nicaragua, Niger, Nigeria, North Macedonia, Northern Mariana Islands, Papua New Guinea, Qatar, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Sao Tome and Principe, Serbia, Seychelles, South Africa, South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands, Suriname, Svalbard and Jan Mayen Islands, Tanzania, Trinidad and Tobago, Tunisia, Turkmenistan, Tuvalu, Ukraine, Western Sahara, Zambia.

This list is reviewed periodically and may change without notice.
