How are payouts sent and how long do they take?

Updated 22 October 2026

GFF pays out in two ways: through Rise (Riseworks), a third-party payout platform widely used in the futures prop trading industry, or in crypto (USDT or USDC).

How do I receive my payout?

Once you are eligible to request a payout, submit the request through your GFF dashboard and choose Rise or crypto.

On EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan, Flex and Instant Classic that has to be during an available payout window. Daily Payouts has no windows, so you can request on any day, once every 24 hours.

We then send your payout through the method you chose. You must have completed the required KYC verification before the payout can be completed.

How do crypto payouts work?

  • You can be paid in USDT on the Tron network (TRC20) or USDC on the Ethereum network (ERC20).
  • You can withdraw up to $1,800 per payout cycle in crypto. The limit counts all your accounts together, not each account separately.
  • Anything above $1,800 in the same cycle can be withdrawn through Rise.
  • Check that your wallet address is on the network you choose. Funds sent to an address on the wrong network can be lost.

The same 3% processing fee applies to crypto payouts.

When is KYC required?

  • EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan and Flex: KYC must be completed before your account is upgraded to the funded stage.
  • Instant Classic: KYC must be completed when you become eligible for your first payout.
  • Daily Payouts: KYC must be completed before your first payout.

The same KYC covers crypto payouts, so you don't need to verify again to be paid in crypto. If your KYC verification is incomplete or requires additional review, your payout may be delayed.

How long does payout processing take?

GFF processes payout requests within two business days after the request is submitted. On Daily Payouts, requests are processed within 48 hours.

Weekends and public holidays are not business days. This timeframe covers GFF processing. After that, the payment goes to your Rise account or your crypto wallet.

What should I do if my payout is taking longer?

If more than two business days have passed and you have not received an update, contact GFF Support and provide your account details and payout-request information so the team can investigate.

Processing fee for payouts

A 3% fee is applied to all payouts, Rise and crypto, to cover payment processing and transaction costs. As a result, the final amount that arrives in your account may be slightly lower than the total shown on your payout certificate.
