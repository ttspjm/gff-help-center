How do I connect my account to TradingView?

Updated 28 July 2026

  1. Open TradingView and go to the Trading Panel at the bottom of the chart.
  2. Select the broker connection option and search for your data feed provider (e.g., Tradovate).
  3. Enter the sim account credentials provided in your GFF welcome email.
  4. Once connected, your GFF account should appear in the TradingView order panel.

If the connection fails, make sure you have selected the simulation environment — not live.
