Are Giveaway accounts treated the same way as normal accounts?

Updated 19 November 2026

Accounts won in giveaways follow the same rules as purchased accounts, except for payouts.

On a giveaway account, the maximum you can request per payout cycle is 2% of your starting balance. On a 50K that is $1,000.

Giveaway accounts also have a lifetime payout cap of $1,000. Once your payouts add up to $1,000, the account is closed and you can't request any more payouts from it.

The $500 minimum payout still applies, and every other rule for your plan is unchanged: the same profit target, drawdown, consistency rule, winning day requirement and payout windows.
