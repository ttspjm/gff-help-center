What are the specifications of each Type and Account Size?

Updated 9 October 2026

EOD Challenge

A one-phase evaluation with End of Day Trailing drawdown. Positions must be closed before market close.

Rules (All Sizes)

  • Challenge Type: One Evaluation Phase
  • Profit Target: 6%
  • Drawdown Type: End of Day Trailing (locks at starting balance)
  • Daily Drawdown (Challenge Phase): None
  • Daily Drawdown (Funded Phase): 2.5% (Soft Breach)
  • Consistency Rule (Challenge): 50%
  • Consistency Rule (Funded): 30%
  • Profit Split: 80/20 by default, with an optional 90/10 add-on
  • Activation Fee: None
  • Minimum Payout: $500
  • Max Funded Accounts: 5

1-Day-Pass-Plan

A fast-paced one-phase evaluation with End-of-Day (EOD) Trailing Drawdown and faster payout eligibility.

Rules (All Sizes)

  • Challenge Type: One Evaluation Phase
  • Profit Target: 6%
  • Drawdown Type: End-of-Day (EOD) Trailing (locks at starting balance)
  • Daily Drawdown (Challenge): 25K 2.4%, 50K 2%, 100K 1.5%
  • Daily Drawdown (Funded): 25K 2.4%, 50K 2%, 100K 1.5% (Soft Pause)
  • Consistency Rule (Challenge): None
  • Consistency Rule (Funded): 35%
  • Profit Split: 80/20 by default, with an optional 90/10 add-on
  • Activation Fee: None
  • Minimum Payout: $500
  • Max Funded Accounts: 5

Instant Classic

Skip the evaluation and start trading a funded account immediately.

Rules (All Sizes)

  • Challenge Type: Instant Classic (No Evaluation Phase)
  • Drawdown Type: Intraday Trailing on Highest Equity (locks at starting balance)
  • Daily Drawdown: 3% (Soft Breach)
  • Max Drawdown: 5%
  • Consistency Rule: 20%
  • Profit Split: 100% on the first $10,000, then 90/10
  • Activation Fee: None
  • Reset: Not available
  • Minimum Payout: $500
  • Max Funded Accounts: 3

Flex Challenge

A premium one-phase evaluation with End of Day Trailing drawdown, no consistency rule on funded accounts, and maximum flexibility.

Rules (All Sizes)

  • Challenge Type: One Evaluation Phase
  • Profit Target: 6%
  • Drawdown Type: End of Day Trailing (locks at starting balance)
  • Daily Drawdown: None (both Challenge and Funded phases)
  • Consistency Rule (Challenge): 50%
  • Consistency Rule (Funded): None
  • Profit Split: 80/20 by default, with an optional 90/10 add-on for an additional 20% of the challenge price
  • Activation Fee: None
  • Minimum Payout: $500
  • Max Payout Request: 50% per cycle (remaining profit stays in the account as buffer)
  • Max Funded Accounts: 5
  • Microscalping rule: not applicable. The 1 minute rule applies, see the holding time article

Daily Payouts

A one-phase evaluation built around payout frequency. Request a payout every 24 hours on the funded account, with no fixed windows and no winning day requirement. Full rules in the Daily Payouts specifications.

Rules (All Sizes)

  • Challenge Type: One Evaluation Phase
  • Profit Target: 6%
  • Drawdown Type (Evaluation): End of Day Trailing
  • Drawdown Type (Funded): Intraday Trailing on Highest Equity
  • Drawdown Lock Point: Starting balance + $100
  • Daily Loss Limit: applies in both phases. Three hits in a rolling 30 day period is a hard breach
  • Consistency Rule (Evaluation): 40%
  • Consistency Rule (Funded): None
  • Profit Split: 90/10 from the first payout, no add-on required
  • Activation Fee: None
  • Minimum Payout: $500
  • Payout Frequency: every 24 hours, no fixed windows
  • Progression Rule: at least 50% of each request must be profit made since the last payout
  • Holding Time: 60 second rule at payout. The 2 minute microscalping rule does not apply
  • Automated Strategies: not permitted
  • Resets: two per evaluation
  • Payout Processing Fee: 3%
  • Max Funded Accounts: 5, counted separately from the other plans

Optional 90/10 Profit Share Add-On

Traders may upgrade an EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan or Flex Challenge purchase to a 90/10 profit split for an additional 20% of the challenge price.

Daily Payouts is 90/10 by default and has no add-on.

General Information (All Plans)

Payout Eligibility

  • EOD: 7 days of minimum 0.2% of the starting balance
  • 1-Day-Pass-Plan: 5 days of minimum 0.2% of the starting balance
  • Instant: 7 days of minimum 0.2% of the starting balance
  • Flex: 5 winning days. The minimum profit for a winning day depends on the account size: $250 (0.5%) on 50K, $400 (0.4%) on 100K, and $450 (0.3%) on 150K.
  • Daily Payouts: no winning days. Your first request is available 24 hours after your first funded trade, then one request every 24 hours.
  • Minimum payout amount: $500

News Trading

  • Evaluation phase: allowed on EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan and Flex. A 2 minute buffer applies on Daily Payouts.
  • Funded phase: restricted on all plans. 2 minute buffer before and after high impact news events.

Platforms

NinjaTrader, Tradovate, TradingView (via Tradovate), TickBlaze, DeepCharts, ATAS.
