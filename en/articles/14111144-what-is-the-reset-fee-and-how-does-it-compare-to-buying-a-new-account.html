What is the reset fee and how does it compare to buying a new account?

Updated 14 November 2026

A reset restarts your account with a fresh balance, profit target, and trading day counter. Same account, fresh start.

Each account can only be reset twice. If you fail after two resets, you would need to purchase a new account.

Reset fees depend on the plan and account size, and new accounts are often discounted, so a new account can sometimes cost less than a reset. For the current reset fee and purchase price, see the pricing page or ask us in chat.

Instant Classic and Instant Lite accounts cannot be reset.
