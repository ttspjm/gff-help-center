What plans do you offer?

1. EOD Challenge. One-phase evaluation with end of day trailing drawdown. Best for traders who want the most forgiving drawdown and room to manage intraday swings. 50K / 100K / 150K.

2. 1-Day-Pass-Plan. One-phase evaluation with end of day trailing drawdown. A daily loss limit applies in both phases, funded accounts use a soft pause, the split is 80/20 with an optional 90/10 add-on, and payouts open after 5 winning days. 25K / 50K / 100K.

3. Instant Classic. No evaluation. Start trading a funded account immediately. Higher fee, no challenge to pass. 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K.

4. Flex Challenge. One-phase evaluation built for flexibility. 50K / 100K / 150K.

  • No time limit to pass
  • No daily loss limit in either phase
  • The 1 minute holding rule applies, see the holding time article
  • End of day trailing drawdown
  • 80/20 split by default, with an optional 90/10 add-on for 20% of the challenge price

5. Instant Lite. No evaluation and no profit target to pass. You trade towards a payout target instead. 25K / 50K / 100K.

  • 90/10 split from the first payout, with no add-on to buy
  • $0 activation fee, live from purchase
  • Full contract limits from day one, no scaling plan
  • End of day trailing drawdown that locks at your starting balance plus $100
  • Daily profit capped at 5% of your balance
  • A consistency threshold that widens as you complete payouts, from 15% to 20% to 25%
  • Resets are not available

6. Daily Payouts. One-phase evaluation built around payout frequency. 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K.

  • Request a payout every 24 hours once funded, with no fixed windows and no winning day requirement
  • 90/10 split from your first payout, with no add-on to buy
  • Full contract limits from day one, no scaling plan
  • A daily loss limit applies in both phases, and three hits inside a rolling 30 day period closes the account
  • End of day trailing drawdown during the evaluation, intraday trailing on the funded account
  • 40% consistency rule during the evaluation, none once funded

See the Daily Payouts specifications for the full rules.

All six are a one-time fee. There is no monthly subscription on any GFF product.

Daily Payouts or Flex Challenge: which should I choose?

Both are one-phase evaluations with a 6% profit target, no time limit and the same drawdown percentages. They are built for different things.

Flex is the freedom plan. No daily loss limit in either phase, no news restriction during the evaluation, and larger payouts when they come.

Daily is the cash flow plan. Money out every 24 hours at a better split, in exchange for tighter rails while you trade.

Choose Daily if

  • You want to take money out regularly rather than in larger amounts twice a month
  • You are already trading profitably and want the cash flow, not the challenge
  • You trade manually and can work inside a daily loss limit
  • You are comfortable watching peak equity, because your funded drawdown trails intraday

Choose Flex if

  • You want no daily loss limit at all
  • You want to trade news freely during the evaluation
  • You prefer fewer, larger payouts
  • You want your funded drawdown to trail end of day rather than in real time

Can I hold both?

Yes, within the account limits for each plan.

Can I upgrade or switch from one plan to another?

Plans cannot be converted or upgraded after purchase. Each account type is a separate product with its own rules and drawdown mechanics. If you'd like to try a different plan, you would need to purchase a new account.

This includes moving between Flex and Daily Payouts. You can hold both at the same time, since account limits are counted separately for each product type. If you are weighing the two up, see Daily Payouts or Flex Challenge: which should I choose?

Optional 90/10 Profit Share Add-On

Traders may upgrade an EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan or Flex Challenge purchase to a 90/10 profit split for an additional 20% of the challenge price.

General Information (All Plans)

Payout Eligibility

  • EOD: 7 days of minimum 0.2% of the starting balance
  • 1-Day-Pass-Plan: 5 days of minimum 0.2% of the starting balance
  • Instant: 7 days of minimum 0.2% of the starting balance
  • Flex: 5 winning days. The minimum profit for a winning day depends on the account size: $250 (0.5%) on 50K, $400 (0.4%) on 100K, and $450 (0.3%) on 150K.
  • Daily Payouts: no winning days. Your first request is available 24 hours after your first funded trade, then one request every 24 hours.
  • Minimum payout amount: $500

News Trading

  • Evaluation phase: allowed on EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan and Flex. A 2 minute buffer applies on Daily Payouts.
  • Funded phase: restricted on all plans. 2 minute buffer before and after high impact news events.
