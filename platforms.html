I'm having trouble logging into Tradovate. What should I do?

Here are a few common things to check:

  1. Make sure you're using the credentials we sent you, not your personal Tradovate account. GFF accounts use separate sim credentials.
  2. Confirm that you're connecting to the simulation environment, not live.
  3. Try resetting your password through the Tradovate platform.
  4. If using the web version, clear your browser cache and try again.

If none of the above resolves the issue, contact our support team on support@goatfundedfutures.com with your account ID.

How do I connect my account to TradingView?
  1. Open TradingView and go to the Trading Panel at the bottom of the chart.
  2. Select the broker connection option and search for your data feed provider (e.g., Tradovate).
  3. Enter the sim account credentials provided in your GFF welcome email.
  4. Once connected, your GFF account should appear in the TradingView order panel.

If the connection fails, make sure you have selected the simulation environment — not live.

What is MDS (Market Data Subscription) and do I need to set it up?

Market Data Subscription (MDS) provides real-time market prices to your trading platform. GFF covers the cost of market data; you do not pay any additional data fees.

The market data included for free depends on your data feed:

  • Tradovate (CQG): the full CME bundle is included (Top of the Book - L1)
  • DxFeed: CME exchange only. If you'd like to add COMEX (for metals) or other exchanges, contact our support team and we will enable it for you manually.
  • TradeSea: the full CME bundle is included (Top of the Book - L1)

Without an active MDS, your charts may be delayed or certain instruments may not load. If your platform asks you to activate an MDS, select the required exchanges and proceed. If you are being asked to pay for market data that should be included, contact our support team.

What Market Data is included in my Challenge?

GFF provides free top of the book market data depending on your data feed:

  • Tradovate (CQG): free market data for all CME Bundle.
  • DxFeed: free market data for CME instruments only. If you'd like to trade instruments on additional exchanges such as CBOT or NYMEX, reach out to our team and we will add this manually for you.
  • TradeSea: free market data for all CME Bundle.

Note: Gold and Silver are listed under COMEX, not CME, so they are included in the free data when trading via Tradovate or TradeSea.

Can I add L2 Market Data (Depth of Market) to my accounts?

We currently don't provide L2 Market Data, however we are working on a feature which will soon allow you to purchase additional Market Data (Including Level 2) from your Dashboard.

Should I self-certify as Professional or Non-Professional?

Most individual retail traders should select Non-Professional. You qualify as non-professional if you:

  • Are an individual (not a company or institution).
  • Use market data for personal trading only.
  • Are not registered with any securities regulatory body (e.g., SEC, FINRA, NFA).
  • Do not work for a financial services firm that requires registration.

Please note that if you self-certify as a professional, you will be unable to trade with us. Professional market data costs $540 per month — despite being the exact same data — and is not something we are able to cover.

If you're unsure, review the CME's professional subscriber definition or consult a compliance advisor.

What happens if I self-certified as a professional by mistake when accepting the Exchange T&C?

If you selected Professional status, you will not be able to use your trading account. We are only able to provide market data and accounts to Non-Professional subscribers.

If this was selected by mistake, contact our support team with your account ID so we can assist with the correction process.

Can I trade on TradeSea?

Yes. TradeSea is available on every plan. It is a futures trading platform that runs in your browser, with TradingView charts, a DOM, bracket orders and built-in risk settings. It also has apps on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Choosing TradeSea at checkout

At checkout, under Select provider, choose Tradesea. TradeSea is its own provider, separate from CQG, DxFeed and TickBlaze Market Data.

As with every provider, you cannot move an account to or from TradeSea after purchase. Make sure it is the platform you want before you pay.

Your login details

After payment you receive an email with your username, password and server, and a link to log in to TradeSea. Keep this email. You need it to connect.

Connecting your account

  1. Open the TradeSea link in your email. Sign in with Google, or with your email address. If you use email, TradeSea sends you a one-time code.
  2. Open the Account Centre and click Connect New.
  3. Find Goat Funded Futures in the list and click Connect.
  4. Enter the username and password from your email exactly as given. If TradeSea asks for a server or environment, choose the one named in your email.
  5. Click Connect. Your account now shows as Connected in the Account Centre.

If the connection fails

  • Your credentials are rejected. Usernames and passwords are case-sensitive, so copy and paste them from the email. Use the login from the credentials email, not your GFF dashboard login.
  • Goat Funded Futures is not in the list. Contact our support team with your account ID.
  • The account says Connected but does not appear. Refresh the page, reopen Station, and pick the account from the selector in the top-left corner.

If none of these fixes it, contact our support team with your account ID and a screenshot of the error.

Market data

TradeSea comes with free top of the book (L1) market data for the full CME bundle, the same as CQG. That covers CME, CBOT, NYMEX and COMEX, so metals and energy are included without asking us to add them.

Other Rithmic platforms

Your TradeSea login is a Rithmic login. It also works on other Rithmic platforms, such as Sierra Chart and R|Trader Pro, as long as the platform lists our server, GOAT FundedFutures. If a platform does not list it, that platform cannot connect to your account.

Copy trading with SecureCopy

TradeSea has its own copier, SecureCopy. You can use it to copy trades between your own accounts. Copying another trader is not allowed, on TradeSea or anywhere else.

SecureCopy copies one leader account to up to 10 follower accounts per group, with up to 3 groups. Your plan's copy limits still apply. Daily Payouts accounts can only copy to and from other Daily Payouts accounts. Instant Lite allows copying across up to 3 accounts of the same program and size.

Two SecureCopy settings need care:

  • Multipliers. Each follower can copy at 1x, 2x or 3x the leader's size. A follower at 2x places twice as many contracts as the leader, so check that every follower stays inside the contract limit for its plan and size.
  • Risk settings on followers. Saving a copy group clears the daily loss, daily profit and position bracket settings on the follower accounts in TradeSea. Your GFF rules do not change. We still enforce your daily loss limit and drawdown on every account.

SecureCopy runs on TradeSea's servers, so it keeps copying after you close the browser tab. That is allowed and does not count as a VPS.

See Signals, copy trading and automated strategies for the full rules.
