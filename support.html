Contact support

How do I contact support?

You can reach our support team 24/7 via:

  • Live chat — available on our website.
  • Email — support@goatfundedfutures.com

You can also join our Discord community for peer support and announcements. The invite link is available on our website homepage.

What is the best time to reach support?

Our support team is available 24/7 via live chat. Response times may be slightly longer during peak US trading hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET).

For non-urgent questions, our help center at help.goatfundedfutures.com has detailed articles that may answer your question immediately.
