What is GOAT Funded Futures?

GOAT Funded Futures (GFF) is a futures proprietary trading firm that provides traders with simulated funded accounts to trade CME, COMEX, NYMEX, and CBOT listed futures products.

We offer four paths to funding: the EOD Challenge (evaluation-based), the 1-Day-Pass-Plan (evaluation-based), the Flex Challenge (evaluation-based), and Instant Classic (no evaluation required).

How do Futures Prop accounts work?
  1. Choose a plan — EOD Challenge, 1-Day-Pass-Plan, Flex Challenge, or Instant Classic (each plan has different parameters and requirements).
  2. Pass the evaluation — if you chose a challenge, trade to hit the profit target while staying within the drawdown rules. There is no time limit.
    For Instant Classic, skip straight to step 3.
  3. Get funded — once you pass (or immediately for Instant Classic), your funded account is activated at no extra cost.
  4. Trade and earn — trade your funded account, meet the payout requirements, and submit a payout request.
  5. Get paid — payouts are processed within 2 business days, through Rise or in crypto.
Is this real money?

All trading at GOAT Funded Futures takes place in a simulated environment.

However, when you meet the payout requirements on your funded account, you receive real payouts based on your simulated trading performance.

You are never risking your own capital beyond the one-time challenge or plan fee.

Can I add a daily loss limit to my Flex account?

Yes. When you buy a Flex Challenge you can turn on the daily loss limit option at checkout. The account then has a daily loss limit, and you pay less for it.

Flex without the option is unchanged. It has no daily loss limit in either phase.

The limit and the price

With the option:

Without the option, Flex costs $119, $199 and $289, and a reset costs $109, $189 and $269. These prices include the current 50% discount. The checkout always shows the price you pay.

Hitting the limit closes the account

On Flex the daily loss limit is a hard breach. If your losses for the trading day reach the limit, the account is closed. It applies in the evaluation and on the funded account.

This is not how the daily loss limit works on EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan or Instant Classic, where reaching it pauses the account until the next trading day. On Flex there is no pause and no second chance.

You choose once, at checkout

The option can't be added or removed after purchase. An account bought without it can't be switched to the cheaper version, and an account bought with it can't have the limit taken off.

Resets keep it. If you reset a Flex evaluation that has the daily loss limit, the reset account has the same limit, and the reset costs less, see the table above.

The rest of the Flex rules

Everything else is in Flex Challenge: specifications, rules and payouts.

Should I take it?

Take it if you already stop trading early on a losing day, and you want the lower price.

Leave it off if a bad session sometimes runs deep. Without the option, a bad day costs you drawdown. With it, the same day can end the account.

What products / instruments can I trade?

You can trade any futures product listed on the CME, COMEX, NYMEX, and CBOT exchanges. Popular contracts include ES (S&P 500), NQ (Nasdaq), YM (Dow), RTY (Russell), CL (Crude Oil), GC (Gold), SI (Silver), ZB (Treasury Bonds), and many more.

The instruments available to you depend on your data feed:

- Tradovate / NinjaTrader (CQG)

The full CME bundle is included.

- TickBlaze

The TickBlaze platform uses TickBlaze Market Data.

- TradeSea

The full CME bundle is included.

- DxFeed

CME instruments are included. If you'd like to add COMEX (for metals) or other exchanges, contact Support and we'll enable them for you.

We do not support stocks, options, forex, or cryptocurrency on GOAT Funded Futures.

If you're looking to trade CFDs, visit our CFD prop firm at goatfundedtrader.com.

What are the trading hours?

Trading hours follow the CME futures session schedule. The main session runs from Sunday 5:00 PM CT through Friday 4:00 PM CT, with a daily maintenance break from 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM CT.

All positions must be closed before the end of each trading session. Specific product trading hours may vary — check the CME website for exact hours on your preferred instrument.

How do I create an account?

You can create a GFF account at any time — no purchase required. Simply visit goatfundedfutures.com and register to access your dashboard. This lets you explore the platform, view available plans, and get familiar with the interface before committing.

When you're ready to start trading, log into your dashboard, select your preferred plan, choose your account size and trading platform, and complete checkout. You will receive your trading credentials via email shortly after payment is confirmed.

How long does it take to get my Funded Account?

After meeting all of your challenge criteria, our team will review your account and activate your funded account within 2 business days. In most cases this is done within some hours, however during periods of high volume it may take longer.

Our commitment is that all passed challenge accounts will be reviewed and upgraded within 2 business days at the latest.

Are Giveaway accounts treated the same way as normal accounts?

Accounts won in giveaways follow the same path as purchased accounts, with one difference: the payout cap.

On a giveaway account, the maximum you can request per payout cycle is 2% of your starting balance. On a 50K that is $1,000, on a 100K it is $2,000.

The $500 minimum payout still applies, and every other rule for your plan is unchanged: the same profit target, drawdown, consistency rule, winning day requirement and payout windows.
