Managing your account

Where can I see my account status and trading stats?

Log into your GFF dashboard at app.goatfundedfutures.com. Your dashboard shows your current balance, drawdown level, profit/loss, consistency metrics, winning days count, and payout eligibility status.

Can I change my account size after purchasing?

Account sizes are fixed once your account has been provisioned and cannot be changed. If you'd like a different size, you would need to purchase a new account.

Can I Reset my Challenge account if I fail?

Yes — EOD, 1-Day-Pass-Plan & FLEX evaluation accounts can be reset twice, at a discounted price. If you fail an account that has already been reset twice, you will not be able to reset it again and would need to purchase a new challenge.

A reset restarts your account balance, profit target, and trading days to zero, giving you a fresh start on the same account.

Instant Classic accounts cannot be reset. If breached, you would need to purchase a new account.

How many times can I reset my account?

You can reset your account only twice.

After using your two resets per account, you can purchase a new account if you wish to start again.

This applies to every evaluation plan, including Daily Payouts.

Instant Classic and Instant Lite accounts cannot be reset at all.

What is the reset fee and how does it compare to buying a new account?

A reset is always cheaper than purchasing a new account. It restarts your account with a fresh balance, profit target, and trading day counter. Same account, fresh start.

Each account can only be reset twice. If you fail after two resets, you would need to purchase a new account.

Daily Payouts reset prices are $79 on the 25K, $109 on the 50K, $179 on the 100K and $259 on the 150K. For reset pricing on the other plans, visit goatfundedfutures.com.

Instant Classic and Instant Lite accounts cannot be reset.

Is there an Inactivity policy?

Yes. If no trades are placed on your account for 7 consecutive days, the account will be breached due to inactivity. This applies to both evaluation and funded accounts, on every plan.

Once breached for inactivity, the account cannot be reinstated and fees will not be refunded.

If you know you are going to be away, tell support in advance.
